One-time or subscription?

Subscription

Monthly or yearly. You get every future version, including SysDial 2 and later. Cancel any time: Pro keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, then SysDial goes back to Free.

One-time

Pay once and keep SysDial Pro 1.x with every 1.x update. When the next major version comes out, you can upgrade at a discount for 1.x owners or keep using 1.x.

A one-time license costs about as much as two years of the yearly plan.

Questions

What happens when the trial ends?

SysDial keeps working as the free version: live numbers in the menu bar and the panel of cards. The window, history and other Pro tools are hidden, not deleted. History keeps recording — buy Pro and it’s all there.

Do I need an account?

No. You pay with your email address, and we send the activation link there. There’s no password and nothing to sign in to.

What counts as a Mac?

Each Mac where Pro is turned on. Reinstalling SysDial on the same Mac doesn’t use another one.

How do I move Pro to a new Mac?

On the old Mac, open SysDial Settings → About and choose Remove from this Mac. Then open your activation email on the new Mac. If the old Mac is gone, write to support@sysdial.app and we’ll free it up.

Does Pro need the internet?

Only to turn it on. After that SysDial checks your license about once a day. If you’re offline, Pro keeps working.

Can I change my plan later?

Subscriptions can be changed in your Paddle account, and Paddle recalculates the price for the time you have left. To add Macs to a one-time license, write to support@sysdial.app and pay the difference.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Use the link in your receipt email from Paddle, or write to support@sysdial.app. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

How do refunds work?

Ask within 30 days of any payment, including a renewal, and you get all your money back, no questions asked. Reply to your receipt or write to support@sysdial.app. After a refund, SysDial goes back to Free within a day. Your data stays on your Mac.

Why is the price different at checkout?

Prices here are in US dollars. At checkout, Paddle shows the price in your currency and adds VAT or sales tax where your country requires it. Businesses can add a VAT number at checkout.

Who sells SysDial?

Orders are handled by Paddle.com, our reseller and merchant of record. Paddle takes the payment, handles tax and issues refunds. We never see your card details.
