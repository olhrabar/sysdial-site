SysDial Refund Policy

Effective date

November 17, 2026

The short version

Ask within 30 days of any payment and you get all your money back, no questions asked. This covers your first purchase and every subscription renewal.

How to ask for a refund

Reply to your receipt email from Paddle, or write to support@sysdial.app. You can also ask Paddle directly at paddle.net. You don’t need to explain why.

Subscriptions

Cancelling a subscription stops future renewals but doesn’t refund a payment you already made. If you want that payment back, ask within 30 days of it. This works for each renewal separately.

What happens to SysDial

After a refund, Pro turns off within a day and SysDial goes back to Free. Your data stays on your Mac.

How long it takes

Paddle sends the money back to the payment method you used. How long it takes to show up depends on your bank or card provider.

Contact

Email: support@sysdial.app. We reply to every email.
