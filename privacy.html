SysDial Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

SysDial is a system monitor for the macOS menu bar, made by Oleksandr Hrabar ("we", "us"). This policy explains what SysDial does with information on your Mac. It applies to both editions: SysDial from sysdial.app (Direct) and SysDial from the Mac App Store. It also covers the sysdial.app website.

The short version

  • SysDial doesn't sell or share personal data, and collects only what's needed to run your license.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The sysdial.app website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't identify you.
  • Everything SysDial measures stays on your Mac.
  • SysDial makes only a few network connections, listed below. The only one that reaches us is the license check in the Direct edition, and it sends only what's needed to check your license.

What SysDial reads on your Mac

To show its readings, SysDial reads information from macOS while it runs:

  • processor, GPU, memory, disk, battery, fan and temperature readings;
  • network activity: current speed and the amount of data transferred;
  • in the Direct edition, the names, CPU use and memory use of running processes and apps.

This information is used only to display it in SysDial and to raise the alerts you turn on. It never leaves your Mac.

What SysDial stores on your Mac

SysDial keeps a small amount of data so it can show history and remember your settings:

What Why How long
System history (CPU, memory, network, internet quality, alerts) The History section and charts 30 days
Watched apps: the apps you choose to watch, with their CPU and memory history The Watched apps feature 30 days
Data transferred per day The Network transfer card about 13 months
Settings: theme, layout, alert rules, ignored apps To remember your choices Until you change or remove them
Free trial start date and your license (Direct edition), in the macOS Keychain on your Mac To run the trial and unlock SysDial Pro Until you remove SysDial Pro in Settings

Where this data is stored:

  • Direct edition: ~/Library/Application Support/app.sysdial.mac/ and the app's preferences (app.sysdial.mac).
  • Mac App Store edition: inside the app's sandbox container, managed by macOS.

To delete all of it, quit SysDial and remove these locations (or, for the App Store edition, delete the app). In Settings → About, Show data folder opens the folder for you.

Network connections

SysDial connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Public IP address. To show your public IP in the Network card, SysDial requests it from api.ipify.org. Like any website you visit, that service sees your IP address when the request is made. You can turn this off in Settings → General → Show public IP.
  • Internet quality checks (SysDial Pro). To measure latency and detect outages, SysDial opens short connections to well-known public servers (Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 and Google 8.8.8.8) and looks up a random name through your DNS server. No data about you or your Mac is sent in these checks; the operators of those servers see a connection from your IP address, as with any connection.
  • License activation and check (Direct edition). When you activate SysDial Pro, and then about once a day, SysDial contacts our license server at api.sysdial.app. It sends your activation code or license token, a fingerprint of your Mac (a one-way hash of its hardware ID that can't be turned back into the ID; because it's linked to your license, it counts as personal data) and your Mac's model name, such as “MacBook Pro (16-inch, Nov 2023)”, so you can tell your Macs apart. We use this only to count how many Macs use your license and to turn Pro off if a purchase is refunded or a license is revoked. If you're offline, nothing changes. It runs on Cloudflare, with the database in the EU.

Apart from the license check, SysDial doesn't send your readings, history, app names or any other data to us or to anyone else.

Permissions SysDial may ask for

  • Notifications: to show the alerts you turn on. Notifications are created on your Mac.
  • Controlling Finder (Direct edition): only when you click Empty Trash in Storage, so that Finder can empty the Trash.
  • Administrator password (Direct edition): only when you reset the Spotlight index. The password is entered in a macOS dialog; SysDial never sees or stores it.

Purchases

  • Mac App Store edition: purchases are processed by Apple. We don't receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • Direct edition: purchases are processed by our payment provider, which acts as the seller of record. We receive only what's needed to issue and support your license, such as your email address and license details. The payment provider's privacy policy applies to the payment itself. We use your email address to send your activation link and, when you ask for it, a link to manage your Macs. These emails are sent through Resend.

The sysdial.app website

To see how many people visit and which pages they read, the website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. It doesn't use cookies or store anything in your browser, and it doesn't follow you across websites. It records the page you open, the site you came from, your browser and device type, your country and how quickly the page loaded. Cloudflare's privacy policy applies.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, GitHub and Cloudflare see your IP address when your browser loads the page. When you download SysDial, our server counts the download by day, app version and country — it doesn't store your IP address. The website has no advertising, no other trackers and no forms.

Service providers

We use a few services to run SysDial. Each processes data only to provide its service to us:

  • Cloudflare — runs the license server and its database (in the EU), the website's domain and Web Analytics.
  • Resend — sends activation and "manage your Macs" emails (from the EU).
  • GitHub — hosts the website.
  • Paddle — sells SysDial Pro as the merchant of record. For payments Paddle is a separate controller under its own privacy policy.

Some of these companies are based in the United States. Where data leaves the EU, they rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep data

  • Your license (email address, plan, Macs and when each Mac last checked in): while the license is active, and for 2 years after it ends or is revoked, so we can help with support and refunds. Then it's deleted automatically.
  • Copies of emails we send: the text is deleted after 30 days; the date, subject and recipient stay with your license.
  • "Manage your Macs" links: deleted when they expire (24 hours).
  • Support emails: as long as needed to answer you.
  • Payment and tax records: kept by Paddle as the seller, as the law requires.
  • Technical records without personal data — payment-event IDs (90 days), unfinished in-app purchases (7 days) and our admin log (2 years) — are deleted automatically.

Legal bases

  • License activation, the daily check and license emails: to provide SysDial Pro you bought or were given (performance of a contract, GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Website visit statistics and answering support emails: our legitimate interest in running and improving SysDial (art. 6(1)(f)).

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, give it to you in a portable format, or stop or limit using it. Email support@sysdial.app from the address you used to buy, and we'll reply within a month. Deleting your license data turns SysDial Pro off on your Macs.

You can also complain to a data protection authority — in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, uodo.gov.pl) — or to the authority where you live.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. When you use the email link in SysDial, the message is pre-filled with your SysDial version, macOS version and Mac model to help us answer; you can edit or delete these before sending. We use this only to reply to you and keep it only as long as needed for that. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Apple's analytics (Mac App Store edition)

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in macOS settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics such as crash reports. This is controlled by Apple and by your settings, not by SysDial.

Children

SysDial doesn't knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If SysDial starts working differently with data — for example, if we add automatic update checks — we'll update this policy and the effective date above before the change is released.
